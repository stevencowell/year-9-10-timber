Industrial Technology 7-10 Syllabus (2025)

The 200-hour Timber course requires all outcomes, at least four documented projects, all Project Development and Timber industry content, and at least one collaborative activity. NESA implementation is from 2028; this is Steve's selected 2027 early-implementation planning candidate. This record makes the whole-course allocation visible without changing the separate 2026 assessment pathway.
