# Year 9-10 Timber course contract

## Purpose and sequence

This site is the shared Stage 5 Industrial Technology - Timber course home. The required pathway order is Breadboard, Small Box, Clock, Tool Carry-all, Mirror, Bedside Table and Folding Chair. Folding Chair remains a provisional/developing option.

## Source authority

- Current mapping authority: `resources/current-stage-5-timber-program.docx`.
- Future mapping authority: `resources/future-stage-5-timber-program-2027.docx`.
- Small Box and Folding Chair future programs are local additions and are not represented as units from the supplied future master program.
- Live project pages provide webpage evidence; they do not prove an individual student achieved an outcome.

## Student evidence boundary

I/P/E means introduced, practised and developed, and evidenced or assessed. Practical competence, collaboration and product quality may still require teacher observation or authorised submitted work. Assessment dates, weightings and Classroom destinations are excluded until verified.

## Release checks

- All seven project destinations respond successfully.
- Local program, audit and support downloads resolve.
- Approved Clock and Mirror product imagery is preserved.
- Desktop and mobile layouts have no horizontal overflow.
- Folding Chair is labelled provisional/developing.
